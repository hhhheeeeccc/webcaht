# Aura Design System: "Obsidian Neon"

## Brand & Style
The design is engineered to evoke a sense of high-precision intelligence. It sits at the intersection of **Minimalism** and **Glassmorphism**, utilizing deep obsidian surfaces and vibrant, light-emitting accents.

## Colors
- **Void Background:** `#050505` (Deep obsidian).
- **Primary Accent:** `#00E5FF` (Electric Blue) - for active states and AI insights.
- **Secondary Accent:** `#39FF14` (Cyber Green) - for healthy status.
- **Critical Alert:** `#FF2E63` (Neon Pink) - for system failures.

## Visual Concepts
- **Health Meters:** Glowing segments representing real-time system stability.
- **Action Cards:** Semi-transparent containers with 20px background blur.
- **Pulsing Indicators:** Neon dots to signal live service connections.

## Typography
- **Headlines:** Inter (Bold, clean).
- **Data/Metrics:** JetBrains Mono (Technical, precise).
